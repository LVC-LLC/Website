ISO in plain terms

ISO, the International Organization for Standardization, is an independent international body that publishes standards used around the world. Its management system standards describe a repeatable framework: define your scope and policies, assess risks, put controls in place, monitor results, audit yourself, and keep improving.

Organizations can choose to be certified to a standard after an external audit. Many pursue certification because customers, partners, or regulators ask for evidence of good practice.

Standards we support

Primary focus

ISO/IEC 42001

The international standard for AI management systems. It helps organizations govern how they develop, provide, or use AI, including risk management, impact assessment, and oversight.

ISO/IEC 27001

The standard for information security management systems, used to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information.

ISO 9001

The standard for quality management systems, focused on consistent delivery and continual improvement.
