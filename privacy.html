Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you use our contact form, we receive the information you choose to give us: your name, email address, optional phone number, organization, areas of interest, and your message. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry and, if you ask, to discuss and propose our services. We do not sell your information.

Where it is handled

Our contact form is provided by Microsoft Forms, and responses and email are handled in our Microsoft 365 account. Microsoft’s own privacy statement applies to its services. This website does not use advertising or analytics tracking.

Keeping and removing your information

We keep inquiries as long as needed to respond and maintain business records. To ask us to correct or delete your information, email info@lakeviewconsultingllc.com.

We may update this page from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.
