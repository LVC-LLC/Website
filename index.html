Why ISO 42001

AI is moving faster than most governance programs. ISO/IEC 42001 gives organizations a structured way to manage AI risks and responsibilities, covering policies, risk and impact assessment, oversight, and continual improvement. It helps show customers, partners, and regulators that AI is being managed deliberately.
